No thesis, no research, no performance — just the low-stakes overflow of a mind that thinks in public. Unfiltered is where a bad night's sleep, a strong opinion about a sandwich, or a half-formed thought that never became an essay gets to exist without needing to justify itself. It's the section most readers will feel closest to, precisely because it asks the least of them, and it's often the one a writer needs most, if only to prove the other sections aren't the whole story.
