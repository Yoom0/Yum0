The honest one, if only because it says what it is right in the name. Featured Partners is clearly marked space for paid or collaborative content — a boundary drawn on purpose so the rest of the site doesn't have to wonder where the line is. Done well, it's a place where sponsorship and genuine usefulness aren't at odds; done badly, it's the section readers learn to skip. Naming it plainly, rather than dressing it up as something else, is what keeps the trust intact everywhere else.
